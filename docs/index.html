About

A trainer for memorizing Rubik's Cube algorithms with spaced repetition (SM-2). The cube shows a case. Recall the algorithm that solves it, then reveal the answer and rate how well you knew it. Cases you struggle with come back sooner, and cases you know well come back later.

Notation and colors match the Rubik's Cube Playground: algorithms use face turns only (U D L R F B), and the U face is white. Progress is saved in your browser.

Keyboard: Space reveals the alg, 1–4 rate it Again / Hard / Good / Easy.
