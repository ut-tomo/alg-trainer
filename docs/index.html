How it works

  • The cube shows a PLL case. Recall the algorithm that solves it.
  • Reveal (Space) shows the answer.
  • Rate how well you knew it: Again / Hard / Good / Easy (keys 1–4).
  • Cases you struggle with come back sooner. Progress is saved in your browser.

Notation is the same as New Takahashi; algorithms use face turns only.


New Takahashi method

Notation

  • U: turn the top face clockwise
  • D: turn the bottom face clockwise
  • R: turn the right face clockwise
  • L: turn the left face clockwise
  • F: turn the front face clockwise
  • B: turn the back face clockwise
  • ' (e.g. R'): counterclockwise
  • 2 (e.g. R2): half turn

"Clockwise" means as seen when you look straight at that face.

Triggers: the only operations you have to remember!

  • Right trigger: U R U' R'
  • Left trigger: U' L' U L
